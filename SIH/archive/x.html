📖 FAQ (Civic Sense)

🚮 Why is it important to throw garbage only in dustbins?
Throwing garbage in bins keeps the environment clean, prevents diseases, and ensures proper waste management.
🚦 Why should we always follow traffic rules?
Traffic rules are made for our safety. Following them reduces accidents and keeps everyone safe on the road.
📢 Why is it wrong to play loud music in public places?
Loud music disturbs students, patients, and elderly people. Maintaining silence shows respect for others.
🌳 How does planting trees show civic sense?
Planting trees improves air quality, provides shade, and helps fight climate change, which benefits the whole community.
🚌 Why should we give seats to elderly people in buses?
Offering seats shows respect and care for senior citizens, making public transport more inclusive and humane.
